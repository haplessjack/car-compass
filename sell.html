Selling safely

  • Don't put your phone number, email or address in the description. Buyers can message you on the site.
  • Watch for overpayment scams: buyers who send "too much" and ask for a refund, or offer to pay by cheque or escrow sites you don't know.
  • Meet buyers in a busy public place and don't hand over keys or papers until payment has fully cleared.
  • Never share codes sent to your phone or email. That's how accounts get stolen.
  • Prohibited: stolen cars, cars you don't have the right to sell, salvage or flood damage you don't disclose, and anything illegal. See the Terms.
