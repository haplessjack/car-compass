Privacy policy

DRAFT, pending owner review. This page is a plain-English draft prepared for the site owner to review before the Marketplace launches. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Last updated: November 17, 2026.

The short version

  • Browsing Car Compass needs no account. The car search, Sources and Rare Finds pages don't collect personal data.
  • If you use the Marketplace, we store only what's needed to run it: your email (to sign you in), your public profile, your listings and photos, and your messages.
  • We don't sell your data, rent it, or use it for advertising.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it at any time from My account.

What we store and why

  • Email address. Used only to send you one-time sign-in links. It is never shown to other members, including buyers and sellers you message.
  • Profile. Display name, and optionally your city, country and a profile photo. These are public, along with the date you joined.
  • Listings. The car details, price, location (country and optional city), description, and only the last 6 characters of the VIN if you give one. Approved listings are public.
  • Photos. Resized in your browser before upload, which removes GPS location and camera metadata. Photos are stored in a public folder so they can be shown on listings.
  • Messages. Conversations between a buyer and a seller. Only the two people in a conversation can read it through the site.
  • Reports. If you report a listing, the report and your account ID are visible to site admins.
  • Technical data. Our database and sign-in provider keep short-lived logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and rate limiting. Your browser stores a sign-in token locally so you stay signed in.

Who processes your data

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Marketplace accounts, listings, photos and messages are stored with Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), currently in the United States (us-east-1). Sign-in emails are sent by Supabase or the email provider the site owner configures. The sign-in library is loaded from the jsDelivr CDN. These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms.

How long we keep it

Listings expire 60 days after they're posted. Your account, profile, listings, photos and messages stay until you delete them or your account. Rate-limit records (when you posted or messaged) are kept for a short time to prevent spam.

Deleting your data

Use "Delete my account" on My account. This removes your profile, listings, photos, messages and sign-in record. Messages you sent are removed from the other person's inbox too. If you can't sign in, contact the site owner to ask for deletion.

EU, UK and California residents

If you're in the EU or UK (GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, export and delete your data, to object to processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Our legal basis is performing the service you asked for (running your account and listings) and our legitimate interest in keeping the Marketplace safe. If you're in California (CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to know, delete and correct your personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Children

The Marketplace is for adults (18+). We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top and note it on this page.
