About Rare Finds

A small nightly job reads a few public auction and classified index pages, the same pages anyone can open, and keeps listings whose title matches a curated list of rare, low-production and exotic models. It honours each site's robots.txt, waits between requests, never signs in, and skips any site that blocks automated visits.

The "why it's rare" notes are short and deliberately cautious. Prices and estimates appear only when the source shows them, exactly as shown at the last check; otherwise the card says "See listing". Listings not seen for 21 days, and auctions that have ended, drop off automatically.
