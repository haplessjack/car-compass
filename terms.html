What Car Compass is

Car Compass is a listing venue. It lets people find cars on other websites and lets private sellers post their own cars on the Marketplace. Car Compass is not a party to any sale, doesn't own, inspect or hold any car, doesn't handle payments, and doesn't guarantee that any listing, price, seller or buyer is genuine. Any deal is strictly between the buyer and the seller.

Your account

  • You must be 18 or older and give a real email address you control.
  • Keep your sign-in links private. You're responsible for activity on your account.
  • One person, one account. Don't impersonate anyone.

Listing a car

  • You must own the car or have the legal right to sell it, and the details, photos and price must be accurate and current.
  • Disclose known major problems, including accident, salvage, flood or rebuilt status, and outstanding finance.
  • Use your own photos of the actual car.
  • Every new or edited listing is reviewed before it appears, and we may refuse or remove any listing for any reason.
  • Mark your car as sold or delete the listing once it's sold. Listings expire after 60 days.
  • Limits: up to 3 new listings and 20 messages per 24 hours.

Not allowed

  • Stolen vehicles, cars with altered or cloned VINs, or cars you don't have the right to sell
  • Fake, duplicate, bait or "too good to be true" listings, and listings for cars that aren't available
  • Requests for deposits, wire transfers, gift cards, crypto or payment before viewing
  • Parts-only, services, rentals, finance offers, or anything that isn't a car for sale
  • Phone numbers, emails, links or other contact details in listings or display names
  • Hateful, harassing, sexual, violent or illegal content, and other people's personal information
  • Spam, automated posting or scraping, and attempts to get around limits, bans or security

Scam warnings

  • Never wire a deposit or pay to "hold" a car you haven't seen.
  • See the car in person, check the full VIN, title and service history, and consider an independent inspection.
  • Be suspicious of sellers who are "overseas", prices far below market, pressure to act fast, or requests to continue by text or email.
  • Sellers: beware of overpayment, fake escrow and fake payment confirmations. Don't release the car until payment has fully cleared.
  • Never share sign-in links or codes sent to your phone or email.

Moderation

Admins may review, reject or remove listings and messages flagged as abusive, and suspend or ban accounts that break these terms, with or without notice. Use the Report button on any listing that looks wrong.
