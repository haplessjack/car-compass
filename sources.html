These are the marketplaces, auctions and classifieds Car Compass links to. “Verified link” means a confirmed make/model deep link. “Browser link” means automated checks were blocked (captcha or 403) but the standard public URL format is still used. “Search on site” means there is no reliable filtered deep link, so the button opens the site’s own search for you to type the make and model.
