Car Compass

Car Compass helps you locate cars for sale worldwide — from everyday commuters to classics and exotics — by building deep links into real marketplaces, auctions and classifieds.

What this site does

  • Search by make, model, year range and country/region
  • Open the matching search on 110+ marketplaces, auctions and classifieds worldwide — including Autotrader, Cars.com, eBay Motors, Bring a Trailer, CLASSIC.COM, Hemmings, AutoTrader UK, mobile.de, AutoScout24, Kleinanzeigen, Marktplaats, Blocket, CarSensor, BE FORWARD, Kijiji, Mercado Libre, Webmotors, Carsales, Trade Me, Dubizzle, Haraj, AutoTrader South Africa, RM Sotheby’s, Mecum and Catawiki. See the full source directory.
  • Search 50+ enthusiast forums and community classifieds (Rennlist, FerrariChat, SupraForums, NASIOC, TheSamba, Reddit r/carsforsale and more). Brand and model forums are matched to your search; most use a Google search limited to that forum, so results are forum posts rather than verified listings.
  • Autocomplete makes/models and decode VINs with the live NHTSA vPIC API (client-side, CORS enabled)

What this site does not do

  • It does not invent, scrape, host or cache vehicle listings or prices
  • It does not run server functions (GitHub Pages is static only)
  • It is not affiliated with the marketplaces it links to
